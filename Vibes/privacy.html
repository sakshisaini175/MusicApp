Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

Who operates Vibes

Vibes is operated by the Vibes team. For privacy questions, use the public support contact provided by the site operator.

Information we collect

Vibes does not require an account. Favorite songs are stored in your browser. When you search for or play content, the app may process the search request, playback request, and basic technical information needed to provide the service.

Cookies and advertising

If Google AdSense is enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show and measure personalized or non-personalized advertisements. You can manage advertising personalization through your Google account and browser settings. AdSense will only be enabled after the site is approved and the required consent notices are configured for applicable regions.

Third-party services

Some music, media, advertising, and site features may be provided through external services. Those services may process information under their own privacy policies. Please review their notices when you follow an external link or use externally provided content.

Social compressor

Files selected in the Social compressor are processed in your browser when possible. We do not intentionally store uploaded files. You are responsible for having permission to use any media you upload or process.

Your choices

You can remove saved favorites from the app at any time. Privacy requests can be made through the public support contact provided by the site operator.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app or legal requirements change. The revision date above shows when it was last updated.
